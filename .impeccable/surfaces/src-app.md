# Surface brief: Huiskamer platform (client and psychologist)

Scope: the whole platform shell and all routes in src/app/c and src/app/p, plus login and onboarding. Mode: Operate.
Audience: psychologists evaluating the demo (buyers), and their clients writing in the evening.
Pinned by the user (24 Nov 2026): "Pinterest aesthetic", latte-beige city people being fancy. Atoma direction dropped by that steer.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Huiskamer as a moodboard you live in. Refuses the AI-beige default (dark green sidebar, colored stat tiles, green-veiled landscape bands, glass pills, eyebrows) by going fully tonal: one latte family, real editorial photography, fashion serif, air.

OWN-WORLD: Bone ground #F6F1EA, oat panels #EDE4D8, latte #CDB79E, mocha #8C6E55, espresso ink #2E2520. No saturated hue anywhere; kinds are dusty tints (honey, sage, blush, fog, mauve). Bodoni Moda for headings and numbers (italic for names), Jost for UI, lowercase nav. Photos in tall arch and pin frames with a warm grade. Flat surfaces: hairline borders, no glow shadows, no glass. Pill buttons in espresso.

STORY: Psychologists see a practice tool that looks like it belongs in a good Gent coffee bar, and still find today, between-sessions and the dossier at once. Clients feel it is theirs and keep writing.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Client Vandaag: left, a large serif greeting with the date as a plain line, check-in as five quiet words with tone dots; right, a collage of two framed photos (tall arch plus a small offset pin) and the espresso next-session card. Psychologist Vandaag: greeting plus one sentence summary (no stat tiles), today's appointments as a timetable, between-sessions as a board.

FORM: User-pinned Pinterest/latte aesthetic (overrides roll 7aab66d7; pick card Atoma-schrift was selected then replaced by the user's steer). Signature: logbook as a pinboard (masonry on desktop) where "Mee naar de sessie" is the pin-save action.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
